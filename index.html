    Transparent AI study coach

    Designing and evaluating a transparent AI study coach to support calibrated trust in university students. I built a prototype to study how transparency in AI advice affects students’ trust and confidence in their own judgement.

    Four conditions

    • No transparency
    • AI-generated label
    • Label with explanation
    • Label, explanation and confidence or uncertainty information

    • CSV data collection and comparative evaluation
